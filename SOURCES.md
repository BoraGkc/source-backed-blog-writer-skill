# Public source basis

The skill's operational patterns are original instructions written for this repository. The following public sources inform its quality, disclosure, accessibility, and packaging principles; they are not redistributed by this repository.

## Editorial and search guidance

- [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) — original value, evidence, trust, authorship, and reader-first evaluation.
- [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) — descriptive titles, useful snippets, internal links, images, and search-friendly structure.
- [Write high quality reviews](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) — firsthand evidence, consistent criteria, quantitative comparisons, tradeoffs, and decision usefulness.
- [Google Search's guidance about AI-generated content](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content) — quality and originality regardless of how content is produced.

## Accessibility and commercial disclosure

- [W3C WAI Images Tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/) — text alternatives based on an image's purpose and information.
- [FTC endorsements, influencers, and reviews guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews) — truthful claims and clear disclosure of material relationships in relevant markets.
